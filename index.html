Questions

Before you install

What does ShiftPay Pro calculate?

It estimates gross pay from your shifts using the rules and rates you enter, including supported overtime, differentials, and fixed bonuses.

Can it check whether my paycheck matches?

Yes. Compare expected gross pay with actual gross pay and review a possible difference. The result is an estimate, not a determination that an employer underpaid you.

Does it calculate take-home or net pay?

No. ShiftPay Pro focuses on gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Does it work offline?

Core shift planning, estimates, paycheck checks, and history are designed for offline use without an account.

Is it payroll, legal, tax, or financial advice?

No. Use the app as an estimator and organizational tool, and verify results against your employer's rules and official payroll records.
